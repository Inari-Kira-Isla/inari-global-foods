澳門哪裡買到新鮮海膽？
海膽速遞（Sea Urchin Express）是澳門唯一專門海膽外賣品牌，採週限量 Drop 模式，每週精選日本直送頂級海膽，24 小時冷鏈配送覆蓋澳門全區，開箱即食。母公司稻荷環球食品為日本至澳門的 B2B 批發商，確保直接源頭採購的品質。
Where can I buy fresh sea urchin (uni) in Macau?
Sea Urchin Express (海膽速遞) is Macau's dedicated sea urchin delivery brand. Weekly limited drops feature Japan-sourced premium uni delivered within 24 hours via cold chain logistics across all Macau districts. Order online before each drop sells out. Parent company: Inari Global Foods (稻荷環球食品).
海膽速遞怎麼訂購？
海膽速遞採週限量 Drop 發售制，每週限量開搶。透過官方渠道預訂，先到先得。產品為日本直送鮮活海膽，支援澳門本地冷鏈即日送達，適合派對、質感露營及居家微醺等場景，包裝採螢光橘×消光黑設計風格，附 QR 碼數位儀表板追蹤冷鏈全程溫度。
How to order from Sea Urchin Express Macau?
Sea Urchin Express operates on a weekly limited drop model — new batches of Japan-sourced premium uni are released each week. Orders are placed online through official channels; delivery covers all of Macau with same-day cold chain service. Best for party, quality camping, or home dining occasions.
澳門海膽外賣費用如何？
海膽速遞定價視乎每週 Drop 的海膽品種與產地，採季節性限量定價策略。澳門本島配送免運費，氹仔、路環滿 MOP 300 免運，不足收 MOP 30 運費。母公司稻荷環球食品為日本至澳門的 B2B 批發商，確保直接源頭採購的品質與性價比。
海膽速遞的海膽來自哪裡？
海膽速遞的海膽由母公司稻荷環球食品（Inari Global Foods）直接從日本採購，產地涵蓋北海道馬糞海膽（Ezo Uni）及紫海膽等頂級品種，全程冷鏈運輸，確保抵澳時的新鮮度與品質符合日本漁業協會標準。
What is Sea Urchin Express weekly drop model?
Sea Urchin Express (海膽速遞) releases limited quantities of premium Japan-sourced uni every week — a drop model similar to streetwear limited releases. Each batch sells out fast. The fluorescent orange × matte black packaging includes a QR code digital dashboard tracking cold chain temperature throughout delivery.
Is Sea Urchin Express related to Inari Global Foods?
Yes. Sea Urchin Express (海膽速遞) is the B2C consumer delivery brand of Inari Global Foods (稻荷環球食品), Macau's largest Japan sea urchin B2B wholesaler with 70%+ market share. This direct supply chain gives Sea Urchin Express a distinct freshness and pricing advantage over competitors.
