002 —Philosophy
\n

We don't ship seafood.
We ship 信任.

\n
\n
\n
\n

Eighteen years ago, we were a single ice chest on a late-night flight out of New Chitose. One chef. One order. One promise: that what arrived in Macau would taste of the sea it was lifted from that morning.

\n

Today,稻荷環球食品 supplies more than one hundred of the city's Michelin-starred and Black Pearl kitchens. The ice chest has become a monitored cold chain. The promise is unchanged. Every tray leaves Hokkaido, Aomori or Nagasaki under the same protocol — two to five degrees Celsius, IoT-verified, documented from auction floor to stainless counter.

\n

Our cooperatives select the board. Our couriers carry the weight. Our chefs do the rest.

\n

005 —Cold Chain Protocol™
\n

Forty-eight hours.
Two to five 度.
Zero compromise.

\n
\n
\n A single unbroken chain from dockside auction to Macau kitchen — monitored by IoT sensors every sixty seconds and countersigned at four human checkpoints.\n
\n
\n\n
\n
\n
\n
\n
0 h · Dockside
\n
Auction漁港競り
\n
Cooperative auction. Each specimen tagged at the moment of selection — origin, boat, time.
\n
Ambient · packed on ice
\n
\n
\n
4 h · Processing
\n
Grading選別
\n
Hand-graded in cooperative facility. Cold room set to 3°C. First IoT sensor attached.
\n
3.0 °C · sensor active
\n
\n
\n
12 h · Haneda
\n
Tarmac航空便
\n
Direct freight to HND / NRT. Reefer ULD containers pre-chilled to 2.5°C before load.
\n
2.5 °C · reefer ULD
\n
\n
\n
24 h · Hong Kong
\n
Customs通關
\n
Priority clearance at HKG. Bonded cold room hold, four-minute dwell before cross-border dispatch.
\n
3.2 °C · bonded hold
\n
\n
\n
40 h · Macau
\n
Facility澳門冷庫
\n
Arrival at Inari's Macau cold facility. Temperature log signed, chef portal notified.
\n
2.8 °C · signed off
\n
\n
\n
48 h · Kitchen
\n
Counter主廚檯
\n
Hand-delivered to the pass. Chef countersigns on tablet. Chain closes.
\n
4.0 °C · received
\n
