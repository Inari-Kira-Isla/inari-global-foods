002 —Philosophy

We don't ship seafood.
We ship 信任.

Eighteen years ago, we were a single ice chest on a late-night flight out of New Chitose. One chef. One order. One promise: that what arrived in Macau would taste of the sea it was lifted from that morning.

Today,稻荷環球食品 supplies more than one hundred of the city's Michelin-starred and Black Pearl kitchens. The ice chest has become a monitored cold chain. The promise is unchanged. Every tray leaves Hokkaido, Aomori or Nagasaki under the same protocol — two to five degrees Celsius, IoT-verified, documented from auction floor to stainless counter.

Our cooperatives select the board. Our couriers carry the weight. Our chefs do the rest.
